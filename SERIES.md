# Itihāsa Through Data

A research series that tests the two Itihāsas, the **Mahābhārata** and the **Rāmāyaṇa**, against independent,
older evidence, with pre-registered methods, open data and stated uncertainty.

**Principles.** Respect the tradition's own term, *itihāsa*. State what the evidence supports and what it does not.
Absence from older texts means "not yet found", never "did not exist". Every paper fixes its method before its results.

**Planned book:** *Itihāsa in the Light of Data* (Book 1: the Mahābhārata; Book 2: the Rāmāyaṇa).
